# Groover

Groover is a music promotion platform that connects independent artists and their representatives with a network of more than 3,000 vetted music-industry professionals — playlist curators, bloggers, journalists, radio stations, record labels, and managers. Artists submit a track and spend credits ("Grooviz", roughly €2 each) to reach individual curators, and Groover guarantees personalized feedback within seven days or refunds the credit. Founded in Paris in 2018, the company also operates Temple by Groover, a fanbase-growth toolset.

Groover runs a consumer web application on a credit-based, pay-per-submission model and publishes **no public developer API**, so the API-artifact tiers of the enrichment pipeline are not applicable. This profile has been enriched with company identity and a domain-security probe.
